# Ahmed Abdellahi Abdat

Founder & CEO at [Hululsoft](https://hululsoft.mr). I lead the engineering: we build websites, mobile apps and business systems, in Arabic, French and English.

## Open source

Our open-source work lives under [@hululsofthq](https://github.com/hululsofthq):

- [Nezzel](https://github.com/hululsofthq/nezzel): save videos, audio and photos from public links on Android. No ads, no account, no tracking. [nezzel.hululsoft.mr](https://nezzel.hululsoft.mr)
- [quran-mr](https://github.com/hululsofthq/quran-mr): a web app for reading and studying the Quran
- [LibreTier](https://github.com/hululsofthq/LibreTier): a free tier list maker that runs in your browser. [libretier.vercel.app](https://libretier.vercel.app)

Merged contributions to [Better Auth](https://github.com/better-auth/better-auth/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Aahmed-abdat+is%3Amerged), [Payload](https://github.com/payloadcms/payload/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Aahmed-abdat+is%3Amerged) [next-to-tanstack](https://github.com/sidiDev/next-to-tanstack/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Aahmed-abdat+is%3Amerged) and [agent.sh](https://github.com/aziz0x00/agent.sh/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Aahmed-abdat+is%3Amerged).
